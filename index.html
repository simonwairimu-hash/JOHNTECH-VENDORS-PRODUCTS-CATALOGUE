Site Preparation Guide

Technical Prerequisites

Essential operating requirements to ensure optimal water pressure, system durability, and water quality.

🛢️

Water Storage Requirement

A minimum 2,000-litre water storage tank is required for optimal operation. This ensures consistent inlet flow pressure and prevents dry-run damage to the high-pressure filtration pumps.

🧪

Free Source Water Testing

For Reverse Osmosis (RO) and Ultrafiltration (UF) machines, Johntech tests your source water for salt and Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) levels free of charge. This allows us to customize the ideal membrane setup for your water source.

📋

Printed Laboratory Report

Standard in-house water testing is completely free. Should you require a formal, printed comprehensive laboratory test report for KEBS compliance or regulatory audits, it is available upon request for KES 5,500.

ℹ️
Technical Note: Please confirm your site's power availability (single-phase vs three-phase) and source water type (municipal tap water vs borehole/well) with Johntech technicians prior to delivery so equipment configurations are matched precisely.
